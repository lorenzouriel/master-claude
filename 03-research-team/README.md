# Team 3 — The research team

**Finds the answers.**

> They read the web, your files and any app for you.

Six plugins that go and get information — from the open web, from your own files, or
from a product you are trying to understand — and hand it back in a form Claude can use.

| Plugin | Role | What it does | Stars |
|--------|------|--------------|-------|
| [REA](#rea) | Detective | Works out how any app was built | 462k |
| [Graphify](#graphify) | Librarian | Turns your files into a map you can ask | 148k |
| [Agent Reach](#agent-reach) | Scout | Reads X, Reddit and YouTube for Claude | 112k |
| [Career Ops](#career-ops) | Job hunter | Finds jobs, scores them, tailors your CV | 83k |
| [Headroom](#headroom) | Packer | Shrinks big files before Claude reads them | 80k |
| [Last 30 Days](#last-30-days) | Trend spotter | Sums up 30 days of posts on any topic | 68k |

---

## REA

`DETECTIVE` · 462k

The most-starred plugin in the whole directory. Point it at an app and it reverse-engineers
how the thing was built — stack, structure, patterns.

- **Repo:** [morluto/rea](https://github.com/morluto/rea)

## Graphify

`LIBRARIAN` · 148k

Indexes your own files into a graph you can query, so "where is this handled?" has an
answer that does not depend on remembering filenames.

- **Repo:** [Graphify-Labs/graphify](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify)

## Agent Reach

`SCOUT` · 112k

Pulls in what people are actually saying on X, Reddit and YouTube, so research is not
limited to what was in the training data.

- **Repo:** [Panniantong/Agent-Reach](https://github.com/Panniantong/Agent-Reach)

## Career Ops

`JOB HUNTER` · 83k

An end-to-end job search: finds postings, scores them against your profile, and tailors
the CV per application.

- **Repo:** [santifer/career-ops](https://github.com/santifer/career-ops)

## Headroom

`PACKER` · 80k

Compresses large files before they hit the context window. Infrastructure rather than a
feature — it makes the other plugins usable on big repos.

- **Repo:** [chopratejas/headroom](https://github.com/chopratejas/headroom)

## Last 30 Days

`TREND SPOTTER` · 68k

Summarises the last month of posts on a topic. Good for catching up on a fast-moving
space without reading a hundred threads.

- **Repo:** [mvanhorn/last30days-skill](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill)
