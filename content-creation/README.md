# CreatorOS

Your content team inside Claude Code. CreatorOS learns your company, your voice
and your brand once, then researches, plans, writes, designs, edits video,
schedules, measures, and archives your content across Instagram, LinkedIn, X,
TikTok, YouTube, and Medium.

## What you get

- **A setup interview** (`/system install`) that builds your base from scratch:
  company, audience, competitors, voice from your real writing, goals,
  channels, cadence, content pillars, logo, colors, fonts, inspirations,
  design guide, brand book PDF, video theme, Metricool connection, and an
  optional import of your past posts.
- **Writing** for every channel: LinkedIn posts and comments, X posts, threads
  and replies, Instagram carousels and Reel scripts, TikTok and YouTube
  scripts, long-form articles and newsletters, all in your voice and passed
  through an AI-tell scrubber.
- **Design**: branded carousels (PNG + PDF), diagrams, thumbnails.
- **Video editing on your machine**: cuts pauses and retakes, brand captions,
  face-tracked camera motion, animated overlays, sound effects, royalty-free
  music, end card, and a quality check on every export.
- **Research**: daily news radar for your niche, 30-day topic deep dives, a
  morning brief.
- **Operations**: an idea pipeline and weekly plan, scheduling through
  Metricool, analytics, a weekly review, and an archive of everything
  published.

## Requirements

| Need | For | Install |
|---|---|---|
| Claude Code | everything | https://claude.com/claude-code |
| Node.js 18+ and Playwright | carousels, brand book PDF | `npx playwright install chromium` |
| Metricool account (Free works) | scheduling and analytics | https://metricool.com, then `/mcp` → metricool → Authenticate |
| Python 3.10+, uv, FFmpeg | video editing (optional) | see `/system doctor` for your OS |

`/system install` checks each one and tells you exactly what's missing.

## Start

1. Copy this folder and rename it to your brand (e.g. `acme-studio`).
2. Open Claude Code inside it.
3. Run `/system install` and answer the questions (20-30 minutes; you can stop
   and resume, and skip anything).
4. Run `/content plan` for your first week.

Day to day, just ask in plain language: "brief me", "write a LinkedIn post
about X", "turn this article into a carousel", "edit this video", "schedule it
for tomorrow at the best time", "how did my posts do this week?".

## Folders

| Folder | What lives there |
|---|---|
| `context/` | Your company, voice, strategy, pillars, accounts |
| `brand/` | Design guide, logos, fonts, video theme, brand book, inspirations |
| `data/` | Your pipeline, metrics, research, and archive (stays on your machine) |
| `output/` | Drafts: articles, carousels, Reels |
| `.claude/` | The skills and agents (the product) |

## Keeping it current

- `/system doctor`: what's missing or out of date.
- `/system update brand` (or `company`, `voice`, `strategy`, `tools`,
  `import`): change one part without redoing the rest.
- When you correct Claude in a way that should stick, it offers to save the
  rule to your context files.
