# Lab universe

> Keeps hands-on articles consistent: the same sample project, dataset, or
> environment reused across articles, so each one builds on the last. Starts
> empty; fill it with the owner the first time a lab-style article is written.
> Until filled, ask for the current lab state before inventing anything, and
> never invent published article titles or URLs.

## Canonical labs

One per area the owner teaches (from the pillars in `context/pillars.md`).

| Area | Lab (project, dataset, environment) | How it's created (script / repo) | Current known state |
|---|---|---|---|
| <pillar> | <e.g. a sample shop database, a demo app, a template workspace> | <link> | <what earlier articles changed> |

## Cross-link convention

Inline after the sentence that needs the background: `... a missing index
(check here!)`, where "check here!" links the earlier article. One to three
cross-links per article, never a link dump.
