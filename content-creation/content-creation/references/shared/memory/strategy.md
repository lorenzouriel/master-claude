## Phase

Building public presence and multiple income streams at once: consulting
projects, a technical audience, and a future SaaS.

## Active initiative: personal brand repositioning (Sep-Dec 2026)

Repositioning from SQL Server/DBA tactical content to "Senior Data Engineer
& AI Engineer", teaching system design, production patterns, real projects,
AI engineering for data people, and tech/career. Four months, one dominant
theme per month:

| Month | Dominant theme | Main pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Sep 2026 | System Design | P3 |
| Oct 2026 | Production Patterns & Reliability | P1 |
| Nov 2026 | AI Engineering for Data People | P2 |
| Dec 2026 | Tech & Career | P5 |

The month's theme sets the weekly theme options. The other pillars still
appear, but the dominant one gets the Wednesday Medium piece most weeks.

Full positioning statement and week-by-week calendar:
`master-claude/projects/Repositioning/repositioning.html` (also published as
"The Repositioning" artifact; last revised 2026-08-11).

## Channels (4 core)

| Channel | Role |
|---|---|
| Medium | Anchor. One article every Wednesday. Everything else that week points back to it. |
| LinkedIn | Primary authority channel, English. Mon-Fri. |
| Instagram | Core channel, posted the same days and themes as LinkedIn, in visual form. |
| X | Core channel (added Oct 2026). Daily short takes plus a reply habit; the Wednesday thread recaps the Medium piece. |

Retired: Substack (was the Portuguese, paid BR-audience layer). TikTok and
YouTube are out of scope.

## Weekly shape (Mon-Fri, no weekend posting)

| Day | LinkedIn + Instagram (same day, same theme) | X | Medium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Reflection: career, tech, goals | 1 post (reflection angle) | |
| Tue | Concept explainer with a diagram | 1 post (the concept in one line) | |
| Wed | Recap of the Medium piece | Thread recapping the Medium piece | Article publishes |
| Thu | Meme-format CTA back to Wednesday's piece | 1 post (sharpest insight from the article) | |
| Fri | Curated list of what Lorenzo consumed this week | 1 post (best item from the list) | |

Plus every weekday: 10-15 substantive LinkedIn comments and 10-15 X replies.
Exact formats, formulas, and posting windows: `references/shared/pillars.md`
and `references/shared/platform-rules.md`.

## Main priority

1. Execute the repositioning (Sep-Dec 2026).
2. Consistent publishing across the 4 channels. The bottleneck is weekly
   production volume, not ideas.
3. Systematize and schedule short-form (LinkedIn, Instagram, X) through Buffer.
4. Site and portfolio.
5. SaaS: planned, not started.

## What can wait

- New platforms beyond Medium, LinkedIn, Instagram, X.
- Large changes to the Medium article format.

## Time-sensitive context

- Weekly volume during the repositioning window: 1 Medium + 5 LinkedIn +
  5 Instagram + 5 X posts (one of them a thread).
- Scheduling and creating short-form content is the task most worth
  automating.
