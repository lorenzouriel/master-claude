# Platform rules (2026)

> Shared by every module. Algorithm signals, format limits, timing, and
> non-negotiables for the four core channels. Cadence targets live in
> `pillars.md` and win over the generic numbers here. Voice rules live in
> `memory/preferences.md` and win over everything.

Reach claims below come from 2025-2026 creator reports, not from platform
documentation. Treat them as heuristics and let `content-analytics` overrule

## LinkedIn

### Signals (ranked)
1. Comments, especially in the first 2 hours.
2. Reactions (PRAISE, EMPATHY, INTEREST weigh more than LIKE).
3. Shares.
4. Saves.
5. LIKE.

### Format
- Hook in the first 210 chars (before "... see more").
- 900-1,300 chars is the sweet spot for text posts. Hard limit 3,000.
- Double line breaks between ideas. Single breaks vanish on mobile.
- 0-2 hashtags, at the end.
- **No external links in the body.** Link goes in the first comment.
- Carousels (PDF document posts): 5-10 slides. Save rate is the main signal.

### Timing
- Tue-Thu 7:30-9:00 AM local is strongest; Mon/Fri 8:00-9:30 AM is fine.
- Lorenzo's cadence is Mon-Fri (see `pillars.md`). Generic advice says 3-5
  posts/week; at 5, watch content-analytics for cannibalization.

### Engagement
- 10-15 substantive comments/day on other creators (`linkedin-comment-drafter`).
- Reply to every comment on your own post within 2 hours.

### Never
- Generic CTAs ("What do you think?", "Tag someone who needs this").
- Announcement openers ("Thrilled to share...").
- Framing LinkedIn as inferior to another platform.

## X

### Signals (ranked)
1. Replies. A tweet with 20 replies beats one with 200 likes.
2. Quote tweets with commentary.
3. Bookmarks.
4. Reposts.
5. Likes.

### Format
- First 5-8 words are the hook (visible before truncation).
- Single tweet: 280 chars hard limit (longer only if the account has a higher
  limit; check the Buffer channel). 71-100 chars tends to get the best
  engagement rate.
- Thread tweets: 140-200 chars each. 5-8 tweets is the sweet spot.
- **No external links in the tweet body.** Link goes in the first reply (or the
  last tweet of a thread). Estimated 40-60% reach penalty otherwise.
- 0 hashtags by default, 1 at most.
- One idea per tweet. Three points = three tweets or a thread.
- Images add impressions; a diagram from Tuesday's explainer works well.

### Timing
- 8-10 AM, 12-1 PM, or 7-9 PM local. Pick one window and keep it; the
  algorithm and followers learn the schedule.
- Space multiple posts 30-60 minutes apart. 8+ posts/day reads as spam.
- The first hour decides reach. Be available to reply after posting.

### Engagement
- 10-15 substantive replies/day to peers, bigger accounts, and prospects
  (`x-reply-drafter`). This is the main growth lever on X.
- Quote tweets: 2-4/week, only when adding a real take.
- Reply to replies on your own tweets within the first 2 hours.
- Pin the best thread; refresh the pin every 60-90 days.

### Never
- "RT if you agree", "Follow me for more", "Thoughts?".
- All-caps words.
- Hedged takes ("I think maybe...", "Some might say...").
- Deleting underperforming tweets (hurts account signals). Learn and move on.

## Instagram

### Signals (ranked)

Feed posts: saves, shares to Stories, comments (depth and length), likes.
Carousels: swipe-through rate, saves, shares.
Reels: shares to Stories, completion rate (80%+ is strong), replays, saves,
comments answered within 2 hours.

### Format
- Carousels: 1080x1350 (4:5). 6-10 slides. Highest save format.
- Reels: 1080x1920 (9:16). 30-60 s for educational, under 90 s always.
  Hook in the first 3 seconds, with on-screen text that works muted.
- Caption: first 2 lines (~90 chars) are the hook. Hard limit 2,200.
- **No clickable links in captions.** Say "link in bio" and what the link is.
- 3-5 targeted hashtags at most. 0 is fine.
- Instagram posts need media. Buffer rejects text-only.

### Timing
- B2B audience: Tue-Thu 8-10 AM local. Lorenzo posts the same days as
  LinkedIn (Mon-Fri).
- Stories are separate from feed reach; optional daily warmth.

### Never
- "Double tap if you agree", "Save this post!" without a reason.
- Captions that describe what the image already shows.
- Two carousels in a row with the same cover layout (check the grid).

## Medium

### Signals
- Reads and read ratio (reads / views) matter more than views.
- Member reading time drives Partner Program earnings.
- Claps, highlights, and responses help distribution inside Medium.
- Medium articles rank on Google and get cited by AI assistants, so titles
  and section structure matter beyond Medium (see `writing/seo`).

### Format
- Title under ~60 chars so it isn't truncated in search results. Subtitle
  carries the second keyword.
- 5-13 minute read (the medium-tech-article default).
- Up to 5 tags. Pick the most specific tags with real activity.
- Set the SEO title and meta description in story settings (`writing/seo`).
- Friend link ("Not a medium member? Read here!") at the top, per the
  article skeleton.

### Timing
- Publish Wednesday morning local, then fire the Wednesday LinkedIn recap,
  Instagram carousel, and X thread the same day.

### Never
- Paywall the only useful part. The free preview must stand alone.
- Republish someone else's sentences (see medium-tech-article rewriting rules).
